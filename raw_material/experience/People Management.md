## Story

**Hiring**： **Getting the right top talent on board:**

- **Needs**： Identify your real needs，Align with org/company's goal, Strategically planning
- **Gap**：Identify Skills gap analysis， home grow or recruit externally
- **Sourcing**： Targeted recruiting， engage in industrial activities， conferences， forum，referral，
- **Selling Point**：Build top talent profiles，sell your org，company，growth opportunity ， competitive compensation
- **Interview**： Standardize Process，skills and Behavioral，Culture fit, and adaptation
- **Offer**： Creatively create compensation packages

- **Case by Case**, junior or senior, technical or management path

    - **Empower**, they make technical decision.

- Soft skill
    - **Effective communication**, feedback

    - **Career path** development

Yiran’s Case (STAR)

**Situation/Task**: When one key person left team, Yiran as a junior but high potential engineer, she has to take technical lead role for one challenge project - upgrade ebay kubernetes clusters into latest version. Since current clusters are running on an very old K8s version, it has security risks. for key application running on these clusters, it also caused velocity and availability issues for application development.

**Action**:

**Technical**

**Trust**: share the whole problem and urgence and critical of this project to Yiran, let her provide the whole plan. it is a complex project and she spliced with multiple phases. She shared some new ideas - for example how to manage patches, how to do verification in testing environments with different combinations.

Supportive: I find several developers to work together with her. help communication with related teams, stockholders, if there were incidents, do not blame her and deal with problem firstly and discuss more how to avoid similar problems, recognize her work and broadcast to customers/high level managers.

Empower: let her decide the execution plan, make technical decisions, break down tasks and run weekly meetings

Strategic thinking: discussed with her on industry solutions, shared with her not just focus on current upgrade, but also how to enhance and help on future upgrading. think it as a platform/product

Soft skill

Effective communication, with 1:1 talk, keeps providing feedbacks

Empathy, understand her challenges and difficulties. work hard and play hard, take a rest when it reached a milestones. do not put more pressure if there was incident.

Career Path development: linked this project with her career path development, she is eager to be a tech leader as k8s expertise. discuss with her current gaps and how she can learn/grow from this project

**Result**:

By 3 quarters, Yiran led team successfully upgraded all eBay clusters (100+ with multiple environments by 10K+ nodes) to a new version. Her technical and management skills have big improvements. She also provided a mature plan (productize) for future upgrade which will reduce the upgrade duration to 4-5 months. She grows to a solid tech lead in the team.

Low performer

Situation, I extend the management for a new team. one team member who joined eBay for 5 years and stayed in current level as a senior engineer.

for eBay senior engineer required strong tech skills - problem solving skiils and quicky learner .

and tech leadership - could drive and deliver one components/service by end2end.

this team member is working on K8s upgrade project. he need complete the lnp testing under latest version and figure out the bottleneck.

However, he made slow progress for the lnp testing, there are metrics drift but he can not figure out the root cause. the project is under risks.

I setup close 1:1 meeting with him - weekly, share him the feedback.

- I told him the clear exception as a senior engineer and what’s current gap.
- I gave him 2 month to improve, and promise to provide supports to him, including
    - find a tech buddy for him
    - provide additional training
    - promise to help him if there are external dependences.

However, after few weeks, I found the progress is still slow.

- He used to work on Ops related work, but have no strong motivation to learn new knowledge (like latest K8s version and how to setup lnp testing)
- When he has unknown, he tried to just ask help from buddy, but less efforts by himself.
- He just got married and want to keep good work-life balance

I shared such feedbacks to him in our 1:1 talk but the progress is not good. finally I have to manage him out and tell him to seek for other opportunity. he left eBay after 3 months.

Grow example

Another story for this new team, another senior engineer (Yiran) who work on K8s upgrade project but she took the project lead role for this project and show strong signal that she is ready for next level - staff level.

at eBay these are a staff level engineer’s criteria.

- more strategy thinking, have strong technical skills to define the project roadmap.
- cross tech influence with cross teams and let them buy-in so work together to delivery a complex product/service.

She started to lead this project proactively, defined the upgrade roadmap, setup weekly meeting with dependences teams, and discussed with them on progress and identified technical difficulties. figureed out reasonable solutions. these are all good signal for her, to show she is ready for staff level.

However, I also found few gaps, for example, the communication with few teams are not perfect. and few internal tasks assigned to team member has slow progress so she has to put efforts and complete these tasks by herself.

I shared such feedbacks to her, and provide communication training to her, let her join the architecture commit, and replaced one team member, finally the upgrade project completed successfully. and she got promoted because of this project.
