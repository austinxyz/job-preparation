# Senior-Level Hiring Physics Playbook

A comprehensive guide for Engineering & Product leaders navigating high-stakes hiring loops. Written for senior IC → Director, Senior Manager → Director, and Director → VP transitions.

Core thesis: Senior leaders fail not because of lacking accomplishments, but because they misunderstand what executive panels actually evaluate. "Hiring physics" refers to the invisible forces — Identity, Language, Culture, Pressure — that govern whether you get the offer.
